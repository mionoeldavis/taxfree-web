## When it doesn't work

If you still live in Germany, Germany can tax the company's profits under its CFC rules or treat the company as German-managed. The 5% only holds when you and the company's management are really in Malta. See <PageLink to="guideLeavingGermany">Leaving Germany</PageLink>.
